Surface Energy γ-Plot and Wulff Shape

Nearest-neighbor broken-bond model. The distance from the centre to the surface in direction n is γ(n): γ(n) = (ε/2) ρ Σv max(0, n·v), summed over the nearest-neighbor vectors v, with ρ the atom density. Cusps mark the low-energy facets.
