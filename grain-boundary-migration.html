MTLE 4150

Grain Boundary Migration

A two-dimensional crystal of atoms bonded by a short-ranged Lennard-Jones potential, run by molecular dynamics at constant temperature. Boundaries move toward their center of curvature, which removes boundary length: an embedded grain shrinks and disappears, and a polycrystal coarsens. Atoms with six neighbors are bulk crystal; five- and seven-fold atoms mark the boundaries.
