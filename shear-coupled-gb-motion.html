MTLE 4150

Shear-Coupled Grain Boundary Motion

A two-dimensional Lennard-Jones crystal with a symmetric tilt grain boundary running down the middle, periodic along the boundary. The thin slabs at the two ends are driven in opposite directions, the left one up and the right one down, so the bicrystal is sheared parallel to the boundary. Instead of the grains simply sliding past each other, the boundary moves sideways: it is coupled to the shear. Quantities are in SI units, converted from Lennard-Jones units with σ = 0.3 nm, ε/kB = 1000 K and τ = 1 ps.

Relative velocity of the two slabs: the left slab moves up at half of it, the right slab down at half. A negative value reverses the shear. Changes take effect gradually over about 5 ps.

3Temperature (NVT)

Langevin thermostat on the motion across the boundary only, friction 1 ps⁻¹, time step 10 fs, so the grains' sliding is not damped. The crystal melts near 1100 K.
